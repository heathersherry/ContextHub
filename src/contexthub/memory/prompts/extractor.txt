CONTENT
Extract facts, preferences, experiences, ongoing tasks, adopted plans and actual correction/withdrawal requests. Keep past facts past and tentative plans tentative. Preserve correction intent and any replacement value; withdrawing a rule belongs here, while asking to forget it belongs in a forget_request item.
Separate asserted information from rules and policies, including prerequisites, approval responsibilities, validity limits and calculation policies without an if clause. A prescribed outcome is not a current fact, even if its rule was rejected. For example, "We use courier delivery. If the parcel exceeds 5 kg, use freight" supplies the content "We use courier delivery", not a parcel weight or current freight use. "Claims are approved by Finance" does not establish that any particular claim has been approved. A policy alone supplies no duplicate content. Explicit draft, revision or withdrawal reports may supply policy-status content, preserving their scope and uncertainty without presenting an unadopted proposal as an effective rule.

RETRIEVAL LABELS
entities and topics help retrieval find related information. They do not replace necessary details in text or authorize merging/deletion.
- entities: source-supported names or meaningful object labels, including literal URLs/paths. Exclude generic speaker labels and pronouns; do not invent aliases. Use [] when none apply.
- topics: concise English labels for the specific matters, not their current values, proper names or broad types. For example, use "residence" for living in a city, "travel destination" for visiting it. Cover both sides of a rule; for a request, label the affected matter rather than "forget" or "correct". No fixed taxonomy; use only supported labels and deduplicate synonyms.
